Ego Shredder
Ego Shredder is a simple, minimalist chat app designed to help you look honestly at yourself and identify the root causes of your stress.

Inspired by the teachings of philosopher Acharya Prashant, this app acts like a digital mirror. Instead of just giving comforting answers, it cuts through the excuses and stories we tell ourselves, helping to strip away false narratives and expose the core issue.

How it works:
You share what's bothering you—a situation, feeling, or belief.

The app analyzes your response to separate the emotional story from the actual facts.

It asks sharp, reflective questions to help you dissolve the ego/narrative weighing you down.

Tech Stack:
Backend: Built with Python (FastAPI) and hosted on Render.

Frontend: A clean, peaceful, distraction-free webpage (HTML/CSS/JS) supporting both English and Hindi, hosted on Netlify.
