# Ego Shredder ◈

A minimalist self-reflection chat app designed to help you look honestly at yourself and identify the root causes of your stress.

Inspired by the teachings of philosopher **Acharya Prashant**, this app acts like a digital mirror. Instead of just giving comforting answers, it cuts through the excuses and stories we tell ourselves, helping to strip away false narratives and expose the core issue.

---

## 👁️ How It Works

1. **Share your narrative:** You input what is bothering you—a situation, feeling, or belief.
2. **Deconstruct the story:** The app analyzes your response to separate the *emotional story* from the *actual facts*.
3. **Reflect and dissolve:** It asks sharp, reflective questions to help you dissolve the ego/narrative weighing you down.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Backend:** Built with **Python (FastAPI)** and hosted on **Render**.
* **Frontend:** A clean, peaceful, distraction-free webpage (**HTML/CSS/JS**) supporting both English and Hindi, hosted on **Netlify**.
